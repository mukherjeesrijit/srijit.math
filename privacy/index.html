Privacy

Last updated 30 October 2026. This page explains what srijitmath.com (the Mathematics for Machine Learning cohort, run by Srijit Mukherjee) keeps about you and why.

What we keep

  • Your name and email. When you sign in with Google we receive your name and email address from Google, and nothing else from your Google account.
  • What you write and do in the discussion. Your solutions, questions and replies, earlier versions of edited posts, the claps, votes, flags and hearts you give, and Srijit's grades and feedback on your solutions.
  • Page views, in total. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts how many times each page is opened. It uses no cookies and doesn't know who you are.

Why

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To show your first name next to your posts, on your profile and the leaderboard, and in the "joined" line on the home page. Your email is never shown to other people.
  • To email you news about the cohort.
  • To see how many people use the site.

We do not sell your information or show ads.

Where it is kept

In Cloudflare (the learner list, the discussion and sign-in sessions). Your browser keeps your sign-in in its local storage. Pages also load fonts and maths rendering from Google Fonts and cdnjs, Google's sign-in button from Google, and videos from YouTube when you open one.

Your choices

You can delete your own posts and replies at any time. To see what we keep about you, to stop emails, or to have your account and everything you wrote removed, email support@srijitmukherjee.com.
