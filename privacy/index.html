Privacy

Last updated 30 October 2026. This page explains what srijitmath.com (the Mathematics for Machine Learning cohort, run by Srijit Mukherjee) keeps about you and why.

What we keep

  • Your name and email. When you sign in with Google we receive your name and email address from Google, and nothing else from your Google account. When you sign in with an email link you give us your first name and email.
  • What you write in the discussion. Your posts and replies, earlier versions of edited posts, and the upvotes and flags you give.
  • Visits. Which pages are opened and when, with a random visitor number kept in your browser, and your email if you are signed in.

Why

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To show your first name next to your posts, on the leaderboard, and in the "joined" line on the home page. Your email is never shown to other people.
  • To email you sign-in links and news about the cohort.
  • To see how many people use the site.

We do not sell your information or show ads.

Where it is kept

In Google Sheets and Google Drive (the learner list, visits, sign-in links) and in Cloudflare (the discussion and sign-in sessions). Your browser keeps your sign-in in its local storage. Pages also load fonts and maths rendering from Google Fonts and cdnjs, Google's sign-in button from Google, and videos from YouTube when you open one.

Your choices

You can delete your own posts and replies at any time. To see what we keep about you, to stop emails, or to have your account and everything you wrote removed, email support@srijitmukherjee.com.
