Syrup and water swapped by the cupful

A vessel V1​ contains syrup, and another vessel V2​ contains an equal amount of water. From V1​, a cupful of syrup is transferred to V2​ and mixed well. Then a cupful (using the same cup) of the mixture in V2​ is transferred to V1​. Now, let r be the ratio of water to syrup in V1​, and s be the ratio of syrup to water in V2​. Then

  1. (A) r>s
  2. (B) r<s
  3. (C) r=s
  4. (D) r>s,=s or <s depending on the volume of the cup.

Source: ISI MStat
