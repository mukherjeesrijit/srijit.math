Two friends driving towards each other

Two friends, one from Kolkata and one from Delhi, start driving towards each other at the same time. It is given that the distance between Kolkata and Delhi is 1455 km. One of them drives at a constant speed of 80 kmph (km per hour), while the other drives at a speed of 50 kmph during the first hour, 55 kmph during the second hour, 60 kmph during the third hour, and so on (i.e., his speeds over successive hours are in an arithmetic progression). How long will it take for them to meet each other?

  1. (A) 8 hrs 56 mins
  2. (B) 8 hrs 46 mins
  3. (C) 10 hrs 26 mins
  4. (D) 9 hrs 36 mins
