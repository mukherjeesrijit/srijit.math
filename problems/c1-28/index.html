Calculus I · Hard · Applications of Derivatives · Problem 2

Filling a vase shaped by x = 1 + y²

Consider a container of the shape obtained by revolving the segment of the parabola x=1+y2, −1≤y≤1, around the y-axis (so its radius is 2 cm at the bottom and the top, and 1 cm at the waist). The container is initially empty. Water is poured at a constant rate of 1 cm3/s. Let h(t) be the height of water inside the container at time t. Find the time t when the rate of change of h(t) is maximum.

Source: ISI BStat/BMath
