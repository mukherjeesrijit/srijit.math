The triangle of largest area with a fixed perimeter

Let p be a positive real and T be the set of all triangles that have perimeter p. A triangle in T has maximum area if its angles are

  1. (A) 90∘,45∘ and 45∘
  2. (B) 60∘,60∘ and 60∘
  3. (C) 50∘,60∘ and 70∘
  4. (D) 120∘,30∘ and 30∘
