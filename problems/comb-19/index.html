At most one handshake

In a room with $n \ge 2$ people, each pair shakes hands with probability $\frac{2}{n^2}$, independently of other pairs. If $p_n$ is the probability that the total number of handshakes is at most $1$, then $\displaystyle\lim_{n\to\infty}p_n$ is equal to

  1. (A) $0$
  2. (B) $1$
  3. (C) $e^{-1}$
  4. (D) $2e^{-1}$
