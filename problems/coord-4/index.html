The length of a median

In a triangle $ABC$, $AD$ is the median. If length of $AB$ is $7$, length of $AC$ is $15$ and length of $BC$ is $10$ then length of $AD$ equals

  1. (A) $\sqrt{125}$
  2. (B) $69/5$
  3. (C) $\sqrt{112}$
  4. (D) $\sqrt{864}/5$.
