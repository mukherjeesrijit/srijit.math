A Bernoulli-type differential equation

Let f(x) be a given differentiable function. Consider the following differential equation in y f(x)dxdy​=yf′(x)−y2. The general solution of this equation is given by

  1. (A) y=−f(x)x+c​
  2. (B) y2=x+cf(x)​
  3. (C) y=x+cf(x)​
  4. (D) y=x+c[f(x)]2​.

Source: ISI MStat PSA 2010, 2012, 2013, 2015
