A differential equation with x²eˣ

Suppose $y(x)$ satisfies the differential equation \[ x\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}-\frac{dy}{dx}=x^2e^x \] with $y(0)=-1$ and $\left.\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2}\right|_{x=0}=1$. Then $\dfrac{d^3y}{dx^3}-y$ is

  1. (A) $3e^x$
  2. (B) $xe^x+2e^x$
  3. (C) $2e^x$
  4. (D) $xe^x.$
