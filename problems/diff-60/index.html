The path of a moving point

The coordinates of a moving point P satisfy the equations dtdx​=tanx,dtdy​=−sin2x,t≥0. If the curve passes through the point (π/2,0) when t=0, then the equation of the curve in rectangular co-ordinates is

  1. (A) y=1/2cos2x
  2. (B) y=sin2x
  3. (C) y=cos2x+1
  4. (D) y=sin2x−1.
