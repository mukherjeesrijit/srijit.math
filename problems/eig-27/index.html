A determinant made of three roots

If $\alpha, \beta$, and $\gamma$ are the roots of $x^3-px+q=0$, then what is the determinant of \[ \begin{pmatrix} \alpha & \beta & \gamma \\ \beta & \gamma & \alpha \\ \gamma & \alpha & \beta \end{pmatrix}\ ? \]

  1. (A) $p^2+6q$
  2. (B) 1
  3. (C) $p$
  4. (D) 0
