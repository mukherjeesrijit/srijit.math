Two statements about 5×5 matrices

Suppose A and B are two 5×5 matrices with real entries. Consider the following two statements:

  1. I. det(A+B)≥det(A)+det(B).
  2. V. trace(AB)≤trace(A)trace(B).
Then
  1. (A) both I and II are true.
  2. (B) I is true but II is false.
  3. (C) II is true but I is false.
  4. (D) both I and II are false.
