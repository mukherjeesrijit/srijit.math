A rectangle inside a quarter circle

C is the centre of a circle, and CA, CE are perpendicular radii. F lies on the arc AE, and BCDF is a rectangle with B on CA and D on CE. If ABDE​=2, then FAFE​ equals

  1. (A) 23​​
  2. (B) 2​
  3. (C) 25​​
  4. (D) 3​

Source: ISI BStat/BMath 2024, UGA Q27
