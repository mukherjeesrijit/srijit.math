Geometry · Circles · Problem 5

Two secants and a right angle

Consider a circle of radius 6. Let B,C,D and E be points on the circle such that BD and CE, when extended, intersect at A (with D between A and B, and E between A and C). If AD and AE have lengths 5 and 4 respectively, and ∠DBC is a right angle, then show that the length of BC is 512+915​​.
