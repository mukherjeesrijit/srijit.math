A convex quadrilateral inside a rectangle of area 2

A subset S of the plane is called convex if given any two points x and y in S, the line segment joining x and y is contained in S. A quadrilateral is called convex if the region enclosed by its edges is a convex set. Show that given a convex quadrilateral Q of area 1, there is a rectangle R of area 2 such that Q can be drawn inside R.
