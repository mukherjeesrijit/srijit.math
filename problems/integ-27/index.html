A Gamma–Beta limit

Let $\alpha > 0$. Find the value of \[ \lim_{n \to \infty} \frac{\Gamma(2\alpha/n)}{(\Gamma(\alpha/n))^2} \int_0^1 x^{\frac{\alpha}{n}+1}(1-x)^{\frac{\alpha}{n}-1}\,dx. \]

  1. (A) $1/2$
  2. (B) $1$
  3. (C) $0$
  4. (D) $\alpha/2$
