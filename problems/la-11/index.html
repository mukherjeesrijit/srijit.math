Matrices with a + c = 0

Let $M_2(\mathbb{R})$ denote the vector space of all $2 \times 2$ matrices over the field of real numbers, i.e. \[ M_2(\mathbb{R}) = \left\{ \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix} : a, b, c, d \in \mathbb{R} \right\}. \] Let $S \subset M_2(\mathbb{R})$ be the subspace defined by \[ S = \left\{ \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix} \in M_2(\mathbb{R}) : a + c = 0 \right\}. \] Then the dimension of $S$ is

  1. (a) $1$
  2. (b) $2$
  3. (c) $3$
  4. (d) $4$
