Skew-symmetric and trace-zero blocks

For a $k \times k$ matrix $A$, $A^t$ will denote the transpose of $A$ and the $(i, j)$-th entry of $A$ will be denoted by $a_{ij}$. Let $V$ be the subspace of $M_8(\mathbb{R})$ defined as \[ V = \left\{ \begin{pmatrix} T & 0 \\ 0 & S \end{pmatrix} : T \in M_3(\mathbb{R}),\ S \in M_5(\mathbb{R}),\ T^t = -T,\ s_{11} + s_{22} + s_{33} + s_{44} + s_{55} = 0 \right\}. \] Then the dimension of the vector space $V$ is

  1. (a) $25$.
  2. (b) $26$.
  3. (c) $27$.
  4. (d) $28$.
