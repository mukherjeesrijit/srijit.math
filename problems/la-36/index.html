Linear Algebra · Hard · Matrices & Rank · Problem 1

Nondegenerate alternating forms

Let ω be a bilinear form on Rm that satisfies the following properties: (a) ω(x,x)=0 for all x∈Rm, and (b) ω(x,y)=0 for all y∈Rm implies x=0. Prove that m is an even integer and there exists a non-singular, skew-symmetric matrix K such that ω(x,y)=xTKy for all x,y∈Rm.

Source: ISI JRF Maths
