Three planes meeting in one point

Let P1​,P2​ and P3​ denote, respectively, the planes defined by a1​x+b1​y+c1​z=α1​,a2​x+b2​y+c2​z=α2​,a3​x+b3​y+c3​z=α3​. It is given that P1​,P2​ and P3​ intersect exactly at one point when α1​=α2​=α3​=1. If now α1​=2,α2​=3 and α3​=4 then the planes

  1. (A) do not have any common point of intersection
  2. (B) intersect at a unique point
  3. (C) intersect along a straight line
  4. (D) intersect along a plane.

Source: ISI MStat PSA 2008, 2009, 2010, 2011, 2012, 2013, 2014, 2015
