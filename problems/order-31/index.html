Identifying the distribution of W

Let $X_1,X_2,\ldots,X_m,Y_1,Y_2,\ldots,Y_n$ be i.i.d. $N(0,1)$ random variables. Then \[ W = \frac{n\left(\sum_{i=1}^m X_i\right)^2}{m\left(\sum_{j=1}^n Y_j^2\right)} \] has

  1. (A) $\chi^2_{m+n}$ distribution
  2. (B) $t_n$ distribution
  3. (C) $F_{m,n}$ distribution
  4. (D) $F_{1,n}$ distribution
