(x − α)P′/P near a root

Let P(x) be a polynomial with real coefficients and let α1​,…,αk​ be the distinct real roots of P(x)=0. If P′ is the derivative of P, show that for each i=1,2,…,k, x→αi​lim​P(x)(x−αi​)P′(x)​=ri​ for some positive integer ri​.
