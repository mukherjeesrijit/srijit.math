A confidence interval for an exponential mean

Suppose Y1​,Y2​,…,Y5​ is a random sample from the exponential distribution with mean μ. Which of the following intervals contains μ with 100(1−α) confidence? Here xk,p​ is the number such that P(χk2​>xk,p​)=p, and T=∑i=15​Yi​.

  1. (A) (0,x5,1−α​T​)
  2. (B) (0,T2x10,α​​)
  3. (C) (0,−log(1−α)5T​)
  4. (D) (0,x10,1−α​2T​)
