Testing an exponential mean

Let $X_1, \ldots, X_n$ be i.i.d. observations from the density \[ f(x) = \frac{1}{\mu} \exp(-x/\mu), \quad x > 0, \] where $\mu > 0$ is an unknown parameter.

Consider the problem of testing the hypothesis $H_0 : \mu \leq \mu_0$ against $H_1 : \mu > \mu_0$.

(a) Show that the test with critical region $[\bar{X} \geq \mu_0 \chi^2_{2n,1-\alpha}/2n]$, where $\chi^2_{2n,1-\alpha}$ is the $(1-\alpha)$-th quantile of the $\chi^2_{2n}$ distribution, has size $\alpha$.

(b) Give an expression of the power in terms of the c.d.f. of the $\chi^2_{2n}$ distribution.
