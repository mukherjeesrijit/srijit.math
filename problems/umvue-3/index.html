Bulb lifetimes: estimating λ

Suppose that the lifetimes (in months) of bulbs manufactured by a company have an Exp(λ) distribution, where λ>0. A random sample of size 10 taken from the bulbs manufactured by the company yields the sample mean lifetime xˉ=3.52 months. Then the uniformly minimum variance unbiased estimate of λ1​ based on this sample is equal to months (round off to 2 decimal places)
