ProblemsNotes

Geometry

1. Triangles

  • Angles: they sum to $\pi$; an exterior angle equals the sum of the two opposite interior angles.
  • Congruence: SSS, SAS, ASA, RHS. Similarity: AA, SAS, SSS in proportion; areas scale by the square of the ratio.
  • Pythagoras: $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$ iff the angle opposite $c$ is right. A right triangle has inradius $\frac{a + b - c}{2}$ and circumradius $\frac c2$.
  • Law of cosines $c^2 = a^2 + b^2 - 2ab\cos C$; law of sines $\frac{a}{\sin A} = 2R$.
  • Area: $\frac12ab\sin C = \sqrt{s(s - a)(s - b)(s - c)} = rs = \frac{abc}{4R}$.
  • Cevians: median length $m_a^2 = \frac{2b^2 + 2c^2 - a^2}{4}$; angle bisector divides the opposite side as $\frac{AB}{AC}$; a cevian splits the area in the ratio of the bases.
  • Area ratios with cevians: triangles with a common vertex and bases on one line have areas in the ratio of the bases.
  • Centres: centroid (medians, $2 : 1$), incentre (angle bisectors), circumcentre (perpendicular bisectors), orthocentre (altitudes). In an acute triangle the feet of the altitudes form the orthic triangle, which the altitudes bisect.
  • Two ladders crossing between walls of heights $a$ and $b$ meet at height $\frac{ab}{a + b}$.

2. Polygons & quadrilaterals

  • Interior angles of an $n$-gon sum to $(n - 2)\pi$; each exterior angle of a regular $n$-gon is $\frac{2\pi}{n}$.
  • A regular $n$-gon inscribed in radius $r$ has area $\frac n2r^2\sin\frac{2\pi}{n}$.
  • Parallelogram: diagonals bisect each other; $ABCD$ is a parallelogram iff $A + C = B + D$ (as vectors).
  • Quadrilateral area: $\frac12d_1d_2\sin\theta$ with diagonals $d_1, d_2$ at angle $\theta$.
  • Squares on the sides: the triangle between squares on two sides at a vertex has the same area as the triangle formed by those two sides.
  • Extremal polygons: among $n$-gons inscribed in a circle the regular one has the largest area (Jensen for $\sin$).

3. Circles

  • Angles: the angle at the centre is twice the angle at the circumference; angles in the same segment are equal; the angle in a semicircle is right.
  • Cyclic quadrilateral: opposite angles add to $\pi$.
  • Tangents are perpendicular to the radius; the two tangents from a point are equal.
  • Power of a point: $PA\cdot PB = PC\cdot PD$ for chords or secants through $P$, and $= PT^2$ for a tangent.
  • Chords: equal chords are equidistant from the centre; a chord at distance $d$ from the centre has length $2\sqrt{r^2 - d^2}$.
  • Crossing chords $PQ$, $RS$ at $O$: triangles $POR$ and $SOQ$ are similar.

4. Area & volume

  • Circle $\pi r^2$, sector $\frac12r^2\theta$, segment $\frac12r^2(\theta - \sin\theta)$.
  • Prism $Bh$, pyramid and cone $\frac13Bh$, cylinder $\pi r^2h$, sphere $\frac43\pi r^3$ (surface $4\pi r^2$), frustum $\frac h3(B_1 + \sqrt{B_1B_2} + B_2)$.
  • A point inside a square: the triangles on opposite sides have areas adding to half the square.
  • Cross-sections: a plane through the axis of a cone gives an isosceles triangle; through two apexes of a double cone, a rhombus.

5. Coordinate & transformational geometry

  • Distance $\sqrt{(x_1 - x_2)^2 + (y_1 - y_2)^2}$; section formula $\frac{mx_2 + nx_1}{m + n}$; area of a triangle $\frac12|x_1(y_2 - y_3) + x_2(y_3 - y_1) + x_3(y_1 - y_2)|$.
  • Lines: slopes $m_1m_2 = -1$ for perpendicular lines; distance from $(x_0, y_0)$ to $ax + by + c = 0$ is $\frac{|ax_0 + by_0 + c|}{\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}}$.
  • Reflection of $P$ in a line: the foot $F$ of the perpendicular, then $P' = 2F - P$.
  • Circles: $x^2 + y^2 + 2gx + 2fy + c = 0$ has centre $(-g, -f)$ and radius $\sqrt{g^2 + f^2 - c}$. Two circles: subtracting the equations gives the common chord; the number of common tangents is $4, 3, 2, 1, 0$ as $d > r_1 + r_2$, $=$, between, $= |r_1 - r_2|$, less.
  • Loci: write the point in terms of a parameter and eliminate it.
  • Transformations: translations and rotations keep distances and areas; a dilation by $k$ multiplies areas by $k^2$.
