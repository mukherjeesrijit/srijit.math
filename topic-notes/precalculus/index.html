Precalculus

1. Advanced functions

  • One-to-one and onto: injective if $f(a) = f(b) \Rightarrow a = b$; surjective if every value is attained. A strictly monotone function is injective.
  • Composition and inverses: $f^{-1}$ exists iff $f$ is a bijection; the graph of $f^{-1}$ is the reflection in $y = x$. An involution ($f \circ f = \mathrm{id}$) is its own inverse.
  • Fractional part $\{x\} = x - \lfloor x\rfloor$; $x \mapsto \{x + \frac12\}$ shifts $[0, 1)$ cyclically.
  • Symmetry tricks: if $f(x) + f(1 - x) = 1$, pair the terms of a sum ($f(x) = \frac{e^{2x-1}}{1 + e^{2x-1}}$).
  • Even and odd parts: $f(x) = \frac{f(x) + f(-x)}{2} + \frac{f(x) - f(-x)}{2}$.
  • Images of sets: $f(A \cap B) \subseteq f(A) \cap f(B)$, with equality for all $A, B$ iff $f$ is injective.

2. Polynomial & rational analysis

  • A degree-$n$ polynomial has at most $n$ real roots and at most $n - 1$ turning points; an odd degree polynomial takes every real value.
  • Multiplicity: near a root of multiplicity $m$, $p(x) \approx c(x - a)^m$: it crosses the axis iff $m$ is odd, and $\frac{(x - a)p'(x)}{p(x)} \to m$.
  • Counting roots in an interval: signs at the ends plus monotonicity from $p'$.
  • Asymptotes of $\frac PQ$: vertical where $Q = 0$ (and $P \ne 0$), horizontal from the leading terms when $\deg P \le \deg Q$, slant when $\deg P = \deg Q + 1$.

3. Exponentials & logarithms

  • $\log_a$ is increasing for $a > 1$ and decreasing for $0 < a < 1$; $\log_af$ is defined only where $f > 0$.
  • $\log_a(xy) = \log_ax + \log_ay$, $\log_ab = \frac{\ln b}{\ln a}$, $a^{\log_ab} = b$, $n^{\log m} = m^{\log n}$.
  • Equations like $c^x = q(x)$: compare ranges ($c^x > 0$ against the sign of $q$).
  • Growth: $e^x$ beats every power of $x$, which beats every power of $\log x$.

4. Trigonometry

  • $\sin^2 + \cos^2 = 1$; $\sin(A \pm B)$, $\cos(A \pm B)$; $\sin2\theta = 2\sin\theta\cos\theta$; $\cos3\theta = 4\cos^3\theta - 3\cos\theta$, $\sin3\theta = 3\sin\theta - 4\sin^3\theta$.
  • Products: $4\sin\theta\sin(\frac\pi3 + \theta)\sin(\frac\pi3 - \theta) = \sin3\theta$.
  • $a\sin x + b\cos x = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2}\sin(x + \varphi)$, with range $[-\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}, \sqrt{a^2 + b^2}]$.
  • Half angles: $\sqrt{\frac{1 + \cos\theta}{2}} = \cos\frac\theta2$; nested radicals $\sqrt{2 + \sqrt{2 + \cdots}} = 2\cos\frac{\pi}{2^{n+1}}$.
  • Periods: $\sin(ax + b)$ has period $\frac{2\pi}{|a|}$; $|\sin x| + |\cos x|$ has period $\frac\pi2$.
  • Equations: bring to one function, or divide by $\cos^2x$ to get a quadratic in $\tan x$; check that both sides can meet (e.g. $2\cos(\cdot) \le 2 \le 2^x + 2^{-x}$).
  • In a triangle: $\cos^2A + \cos^2B + \cos^2C + 2\cos A\cos B\cos C = 1$.

5. Complex numbers & polar coordinates

  • $z = x + iy = re^{i\theta}$; $|z_1z_2| = |z_1||z_2|$, $\arg(z_1z_2) = \arg z_1 + \arg z_2$; $z\bar z = |z|^2$.
  • De Moivre: $(\cos\theta + i\sin\theta)^n = \cos n\theta + i\sin n\theta$; e.g. $(\sqrt3 + i)^6 = 2^6e^{i\pi} = -64$.
  • Roots of unity: the roots of $z^n = 1$ are $e^{2\pi ik/n}$; they add to $0$, and $\prod(x - \omega^k) = x^n - 1$.
  • Geometry: $|z - a| = |z - b|$ is a perpendicular bisector; $|z - a| + |z - b| = 2c$ is an ellipse (a segment if $2c = |a - b|$); $|z_1 \pm z_2|^2 = |z_1|^2 + |z_2|^2 \pm 2\mathrm{Re}(z_1\bar z_2)$.
  • Lines: $az + b\bar z + c = 0$ is a line iff $|a| = |b| \ne 0$ and $\bar ac = b\bar c$.
  • Polar curves: $r = a\cos\theta$ is the circle $x^2 + y^2 = ax$.

6. Vectors & matrices

  • Dot product $u\cdot v = |u||v|\cos\theta$; cross product $|u \times v| = |u||v|\sin\theta$, perpendicular to both.
  • Lines and planes: a plane through a point with normal $n$ is $n\cdot(x - x_0) = 0$; planes through the line of intersection of $P_1 = 0$ and $P_2 = 0$ are $P_1 + \lambda P_2 = 0$.
  • Determinants: $\det(AB) = \det A\det B$, $\det(A^n) = (\det A)^n$, $\det(kA) = k^n\det A$; row operations and expansion along a row; a matrix is invertible iff $\det \ne 0$.
  • Matrix algebra: $(A + B)^2 = A^2 + AB + BA + B^2$, so it equals $A^2 + B^2$ iff $AB + BA = 0$. $A^{-1} = \frac{\mathrm{adj}A}{\det A}$.
  • Rotations: $\begin{pmatrix} \cos\theta & -\sin\theta \\ \sin\theta & \cos\theta \end{pmatrix}^n$ is rotation by $n\theta$.
  • Cramer's rule: $x_i = \frac{\det A_i}{\det A}$.

7. Conic sections, induction & limits

  • Parabola $y^2 = 4ax$: tangent at $(at^2, 2at)$ is $ty = x + at^2$; chord of contact from $(h, k)$ is $ky = 2a(x + h)$.
  • Ellipse $\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$: $y = mx + c$ is tangent iff $c^2 = a^2m^2 + b^2$. Hyperbola $\frac{x^2}{a^2} - \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1$: $c^2 = a^2m^2 - b^2$.
  • Classifying $ax^2 + 2hxy + by^2 + \dots = 0$: ellipse, parabola or hyperbola as $h^2 - ab <, =, > 0$ (or degenerate: a point, lines).
  • Confocal conics $\frac{x^2}{a^2 + \lambda} + \frac{y^2}{b^2 + \lambda} = 1$: eliminate $\lambda$ by differentiating.
  • Induction: prove $P(1)$ and $P(n) \Rightarrow P(n + 1)$; strong induction assumes all smaller cases.
  • Limits: $\frac{\sin x}{x} \to 1$, $\left(1 + \frac an\right)^n \to e^a$; a limit of $\cos^n(n^{-x})$ is found from $n\log\cos(n^{-x}) \approx -\frac{n^{1-2x}}{2}$.
