Probability

1. Counting and elementary probability

  • Classical probability: $P(A)=\frac{|A|}{|\Omega|}$ when all outcomes are equally likely.
  • Permutations and combinations: $n!$ arrangements; $\binom nk$ choices; multinomial $\frac{n!}{k_1!\cdots k_r!}$.
  • Stars and bars: nonnegative integer solutions of $x_1+\dots+x_k=n$: $\binom{n+k-1}{k-1}$.
  • Inclusion–exclusion: $P(\cup A_i)=\sum P(A_i)-\sum P(A_i\cap A_j)+\cdots$. "At least one of each colour" $=1-P(\text{some colour missing})$.
  • Derangements: $D_n=n!\sum_{k=0}^n\frac{(-1)^k}{k!}\approx\frac{n!}e$.
  • Geometric probability: probability $=\frac{\text{favourable length/area}}{\text{total}}$.

Conditional probability and independence

  • $P(A\mid B)=\frac{P(A\cap B)}{P(B)}$; multiplication rule $P(A\cap B)=P(B)P(A\mid B)$.
  • Total probability: $P(A)=\sum P(A\mid B_i)P(B_i)$ for a partition $\{B_i\}$.
  • Bayes: $P(B_j\mid A)=\frac{P(A\mid B_j)P(B_j)}{\sum_iP(A\mid B_i)P(B_i)}$. (Typical: test sensitivity and prevalence.)
  • $A,B$ independent iff $P(A\cap B)=P(A)P(B)$. Pairwise independence does not imply mutual independence.

2. Random variables and expectation

  • CDF: $F(x)=P(X\le x)$: nondecreasing, right-continuous, $F(-\infty)=0$, $F(\infty)=1$. $P(a<X\le b)=F(b)-F(a)$.
  • Discrete: pmf $p(x)$; continuous: density $f=F'$ with $P(a<X<b)=\int_a^bf$.
  • Expectation: $E[g(X)]=\sum g(x)p(x)$ or $\int g(x)f(x)\,dx$. Linear: $E[aX+bY]=aE[X]+bE[Y]$ always.
  • Tail formula: for $X\ge0$, $E[X]=\int_0^\infty P(X>t)\,dt$; for integer $X\ge0$, $E[X]=\sum_{k\ge1}P(X\ge k)$.
  • Variance: $\operatorname{Var}X=E[X^2]-(EX)^2$, $\operatorname{Var}(aX+b)=a^2\operatorname{Var}X$. $E(X-c)^2=\operatorname{Var}X+(EX-c)^2$.
  • Indicators: $X=\sum\mathbf 1_{A_i}$ gives $E[X]=\sum P(A_i)$. (Expected fixed points of a random permutation: $1$.)
  • Moment generating function: $M(t)=E[e^{tX}]$; $M^{(k)}(0)=E[X^k]$; it determines the distribution.

Inequalities

  • Markov: $P(X\ge a)\le\frac{E[X]}a$ for $X\ge0$.
  • Chebyshev: $P(|X-\mu|\ge k)\le\frac{\sigma^2}{k^2}$, so $P(|X-\mu|<k)\ge1-\frac{\sigma^2}{k^2}$.
  • Jensen: $E[\varphi(X)]\ge\varphi(E[X])$ for convex $\varphi$.

  • Memoryless: $P(X>s+t\mid X>s)=P(X>t)$ for the exponential (and geometric).
  • Transformations: if $Y=g(X)$ with $g$ monotone, $f_Y(y)=f_X(g^{-1}(y))\left|\frac{d}{dy}g^{-1}(y)\right|$. If $U\sim$ Uniform$(0,1)$ then $-\log U\sim$ Exp$(1)$, and $F^{-1}(U)$ has CDF $F$.

4. Joint distributions

  • Joint density $f(x,y)$; marginal $f_X(x)=\int f(x,y)\,dy$; conditional $f_{Y\mid X}(y\mid x)=\frac{f(x,y)}{f_X(x)}$.
  • Independence: $f(x,y)=f_X(x)f_Y(y)$ (the support must be a product set).
  • Covariance: $\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=E[XY]-E[X]E[Y]$; $\operatorname{Var}(X+Y)=\operatorname{Var}X+\operatorname{Var}Y+2\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)$; correlation $\rho\in[-1,1]$. Independent $\Rightarrow$ uncorrelated, not conversely (except jointly normal).
  • Conditional expectation: $E[E[Y\mid X]]=E[Y]$; $\operatorname{Var}Y=E[\operatorname{Var}(Y\mid X)]+\operatorname{Var}(E[Y\mid X])$.
  • Sums: the density of $X+Y$ (independent) is the convolution $\int f_X(t)f_Y(z-t)\,dt$. Sums of independent Poissons, normals, gammas (same rate) stay in the family.
  • Order statistics of an i.i.d. sample with CDF $F$: $P(X_{(n)}\le x)=F(x)^n$, $P(X_{(1)}>x)=(1-F(x))^n$. The minimum of $n$ i.i.d. Exp$(\lambda)$ is Exp$(n\lambda)$.
  • Change of variables: $f_{U,V}(u,v)=f_{X,Y}(x,y)\left|\det\frac{\partial(x,y)}{\partial(u,v)}\right|$.

Multivariate normal and sampling distributions

  • Linear combinations of a jointly normal vector are normal; for jointly normal variables, uncorrelated means independent.
  • If $X_1,\dots,X_n$ are i.i.d. $N(\mu,\sigma^2)$: $\bar X\sim N(\mu,\frac{\sigma^2}n)$, $\frac{(n-1)S^2}{\sigma^2}\sim\chi^2_{n-1}$, and $\bar X$, $S^2$ are independent.
  • $\chi^2_k$ is a sum of $k$ squared standard normals; $t_k=\frac{Z}{\sqrt{V/k}}$; $F_{m,n}=\frac{U/m}{V/n}$ (independent $\chi^2$'s).

5. Limit theorems

  • Convergence in probability: $P(|X_n-X|>\epsilon)\to0$. In distribution: $F_n(x)\to F(x)$ at continuity points of $F$.
  • Weak law of large numbers: for i.i.d. with finite mean $\mu$, $\bar X_n\to\mu$ in probability (Chebyshev gives it when the variance is finite). So $\frac1n\sum g(X_i)\to E[g(X)]$.
  • Central limit theorem: for i.i.d. with mean $\mu$ and variance $\sigma^2$, $\frac{\sqrt n(\bar X_n-\mu)}{\sigma}\to N(0,1)$ in distribution. E.g. for Exp$(1)$ sums, $P(S_n>n)\to\frac12$; for $\chi^2_n$, $P(\chi^2_n>n+\sqrt{2n})\to P(Z>1)\approx0.1587$.
  • Continuous mapping: if $X_n\to X$ then $g(X_n)\to g(X)$ for continuous $g$. Slutsky: if $X_n\to X$ in distribution and $Y_n\to c$, then $X_n+Y_n\to X+c$ and $X_nY_n\to cX$.
  • Delta method: $\sqrt n(g(\bar X_n)-g(\mu))\to N(0,g'(\mu)^2\sigma^2)$.
